The first release of the native PC port of *Harvest Moon: Animal Parade* (Wii).

**No game data is included.** You need your own disc: `Harvest Moon: Animal Parade (USA)`, `RBIEE9`.

### Download

- **Windows x64**: `HarvestMoonAnimalParade-windows-x64.zip`
- **Windows on ARM** (Snapdragon X): `HarvestMoonAnimalParade-windows-arm64.zip`

Unzip it to a short folder (for example `C:\HMAP`), double click `play.cmd`, choose your disc image (.rvz, .iso or .wbfs) and prepare the game. The first time takes several minutes (it extracts the disc, translates the game and compiles it); after that, **Play** starts it right away.

### What's in it

- The game runs natively on Windows x64 and Windows on ARM, with no emulator.
- A launcher to prepare and start the game, with its main settings and every control.
- Out of the box it looks and plays as on the Wii. You can choose:
  - a wide picture;
  - frame interpolation (60, 120 or 180 FPS);
  - higher resolutions;
  - Direct3D 12 or Vulkan.
- Fast loading is always on. Holding a key or a controller button fast-forwards everything (off by default).
- Five languages from the US disc: English, Spanish, French, German and Italian.
- An emulated Wii Remote and Nunchuk on a gamepad or the keyboard, with the mouse as the pointer. A real Wii Remote can also be paired.

Known issue: in languages other than English the title screen shows the Japanese logo.
